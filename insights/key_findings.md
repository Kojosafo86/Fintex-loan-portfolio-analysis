# Key findings

The findings, tables and charts now live in the main [README](../README.md),
with every figure produced by the SQL in [`sql/`](../sql) and saved to
[`results/`](../results).

In one line: the matured book collected 1.07 per 1.00 lent with a 4.7%
principal loss rate; losses fell from 14.7% (2020-Q3) to ~2-3% as the book
shifted to proven repeat borrowers, and 4-6 instalment loans lose about 6x more
than shorter loans of the same size.
